INTERVAL: a teletext service, rendered on a simulated television

Pages of live text: news, most read articles, technology, on this day, weather, the moon, space weather, earthquakes, and the listings for the SIGNAL radio network. Each page is announced as it arrives.

Keys: P switches the set on or off. Type a three digit page number to go to a page; 100 is the index and 199 is help. Up and down arrows go to the next or previous page, left and right step through subpages. F1 to F4, or Shift and 1 to 4, follow the four coloured links. I goes to the index. R reveals hidden answers. H holds the page that is up. S changes the size. C changes between colour, black and white, and a monitor. N turns the overnight pages on or off, M mutes the overnight music, F is full screen, and Escape clears a half-keyed number.
