INTERVAL: a teletext service, rendered on a simulated television

Bite-sized pages of live text in seven sections: news, today, weather, money, a pause, sport and a gallery. Each page is announced as it arrives.

Keys: P switches the set on or off. Type a three digit page number to go to a page; 100 is the index and 199 is help. Up and down arrows go to the next or previous page, left and right step through subpages. F1 to F4, or Shift and 1 to 4, follow the four coloured links. I goes to the index, and a question mark to help. H holds the page that is up. C changes between colour, black and white, and a monitor. N turns cycling on or off, so the set goes through the sections by itself; while it does, H holds the section. F is full screen, and Escape clears a half-keyed number or gives up a search.
